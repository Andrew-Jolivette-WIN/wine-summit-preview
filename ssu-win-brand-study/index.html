The focus

The industry’s challenges are well documented. This series asks what is working.

Across the wine industry, businesses are finding new ways to reach consumers, demonstrate value, manage change, adopt technology, and build resilience. The Wine Business Innovation Summit Series brings those ideas forward so others can learn from them.
